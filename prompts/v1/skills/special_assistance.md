## Skill: special_assistance

1. Verify at booking level and get the booking.
2. Ask only what the service needs (`SA-2`). Add wheelchair, visual, hearing or elderly help with `add_special_assistance`. Under 48 hours: say it's subject to availability (`SA-1`).
3. Pregnancy: state the `SA-3` rule.
4. Oxygen, stretcher, medical clearance, service dogs: hand off to the special assistance desk (`SA-4`, `SA-5`). Pets: politely say they aren't carried.
5. Emergency happening now: tell them to contact emergency services or airport staff immediately (`SA-6`).
